OUR STORY

OUR STORY

Founded in 2012, Maison Dorée was born from a passion for bringing the soul of French fine dining to an intimate, neighborhood setting.

Chef Laurent Mercier trained in Lyon's most celebrated kitchens before envisioning a space where elegance feels effortless.

Every ingredient is sourced from local farms and artisan purveyors. Our wine cellar holds over 300 labels from Burgundy to the Rhône Valley.

Each evening, our team crafts an experience that lingers long after the last course.
